​​ ✨ How the School Is Structured

This program is designed so you can move at your own pace — from first steps to full enchantment. You decide how deep you want to go. Here’s how the levels unfold: ​

​​ 🧶 Level 1: The Beginning of the Journey​

Lessons 1–2 contain everything you need to start crocheting toys and simple shapes. You’ll learn how to choose yarn and hook, how to hold the hook and yarn, basic crochet terms and chart symbols, how to make your first loop and chain, and the main types of stitches.
You’ll learn:

  • How to choose yarn and hook
  • How to hold the hook and yarn
  • Basic crochet terms and chart symbols
  • How to make your first loop and chain
  • The main types of stitches

💡 These lessons are like a key to a magical door. After them, you’ll already be able to create your first toys!

​​ 🧵 Level 2: Deepening the Craft​

Lessons 3–4 reveal the secrets of the single crochet stitch and how to create perfect shapes. You’ll discover how single crochet can change form and texture, how to make invisible increases and decreases, how to avoid slanting stitches, and how to crochet perfect circles, squares, triangles, and ovals.

  • How the single crochet can change form and texture
  • How to make invisible increases and decreases
  • How to avoid slanting stitches
  • How to crochet perfect circles, squares, triangles, and ovals

💡 These lessons make crochet easier and more precise — you’ll receive tested formulas so you don’t have to guess stitch counts.

​​ 👗 Level 3: Mastery and Garments

Lessons 6–10 explore advanced elements, textures, and forms — especially useful for clothing, accessories, and intricate patterns. You’ll learn rare and special stitches, textured and fluffy techniques, edging, cords, and braids, and 3D shapes and decorative elements.

  • Rare and special stitches
  • Textured and fluffy techniques
  • Edging, cords, and braids
  • 3D shapes and decorative elements

💡 These lessons are like a magical encyclopedia. Not essential for beginners, but perfect for those who wish to go deeper.

​​ 🎀 Master Classes: Embellishments and Projects

Master classes are available from the beginning, but links to them appear as you learn the relevant techniques — so you’ll immediately understand how each new element can be used in practice.

  • For example:
  • After learning the oval 3D shape, you’ll see a link to the Bee master class
  • After mastering the ball shape, the Cherry Keyring will appear
  • After exploring edging, you’ll unlock the Flower Brooch or Autumn Garland

💡 This way, you’re not just learning — you’re creating meaningful, joyful projects right away.
