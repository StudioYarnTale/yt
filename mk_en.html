💖 We truly value what we’ve poured our time, soul, and imagination into. Handcrafting is not just a skill — it’s character-building. It nurtures patience, perseverance, focus, and creative thinking. This shines especially bright when a child reaches the level where they begin to invent.

That’s exactly how our school is built. Yes, it offers ready-made blocks — like a construction set. But it goes far deeper than a step-by-step master class. We don’t begin with instructions — we begin with imagination.

🧶 Master Classes — From Skill to Creativity

Each master class is not just a set of instructions — it’s an invitation to create. Its goal is to give children (or adults) the skills and knowledge that become the foundation for independent invention. We don’t simply repeat — we learn to create.

Every element learned in a master class can later be changed, transformed, and adapted. It’s not a rigid formula, but a flexible tool that grows with the learner.

✨ What Does Crochet Develop?

  • Crochet is not only about aesthetics — it’s also about technical thinking. It teaches how to:
  • calculate the amount of yarn needed
  • choose the right hook for the yarn
  • understand and build stitch charts
  • join parts with precision and logic
  • work with colour: which shades harmonize and which clash
  • give a face expression — a cheerful squirrel, a sad piglet
  • assess proportions of a toy
  • design clothing and adjust it to fit

Here we find math, logic, painting, and even psychology. Because to express emotion through a toy, you must first feel it.

🎨 From Stitch Chart to Story

  • Before we begin crocheting, we invite the child to:
  • draw the future toy
  • imagine its personality, habits, and favourite activities
  • describe its outfit
  • invent a role-playing game
  • and then — write a scene or a whole fairy tale.
