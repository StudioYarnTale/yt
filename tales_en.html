🌳 Tales from the Forest of Feathers and Thorns 🌳

        These stories are woven from gentle strangeness, quiet wisdom, and playful mischief. They may be especially loved by children aged around 9 and older. Here you’ll find a concert in a poultry yard organised by an activist rabbit, a curious chick, a daring gosling, a poetic dragonfly, and even a manipulative cactus and an ever-surviving cockroach. ​

        There’s laughter and action, yes—but also truths that may not be grasped at once. Some lessons hide between the lines, waiting to bloom when the reader grows older. These tales are meant to be remembered, revisited, and slowly understood—like forest echoes that return when the heart is ready. ​

        The tales were purred into being by Philomur the Cat, who never worries whether a story will be liked—he simply murmurs whatever comes to mind, curled by the fire with a book and a cup of warm milk. And they were faithfully recorded by Michel the Rat, his peculiar but devoted friend, who always carries a net bag of oranges and his old-fashioned notebook wherever he goes. Together, they offer you this collection—not perfect, not polished, but full of heart, feathers, and thorns.

🔶 The Adventures of Chick Peep 🐣
Born in the quiet of an old barn, a tiny chick meets a spider — and learns that even grumpiness can hide gentle kindness.
Recommended for readers aged 9–10 and older.



🔶 A story of a broken window and friendship 🐷
Truth, no matter how difficult it may be, can become a bridge that turns resentment into friendship and injustice into warmth.
Recommended for readers aged 9–10 and older.



🔶 The Gracious Hedgehog 🦔
A forest parable about kindness that shines freely and asks for nothing in return.
Recommended for readers aged 10–11 and older.



🔶 The Long Road Home 🪿
Through danger and long miles, a little gosling finds the way home, guided by the quiet miracle of a mother’s love that never fades.
Recommended for readers aged 10–11 and older.



🔶 Long-Awaited Guest 🦉
Those who remember their roots will always find their way back to themselves.
Recommended for readers aged 10–11 and older.



🔶 The Grand Concert in the Poultry Yard 🐰
Rabbit Maxim takes charge! When lofty ideals meet feathers and fuss, an ordinary concert turns into a real campaign — and dancing becomes a manifesto.
Recommended for readers aged 10–11 and older.



🔶 ️ A Webbed Tale 🕸
In the silence of unreturned love, an old, reclusive weaver creates his final gift. Only sacrifice can awaken a young lady’s heart to what truly matters.
Recommended for readers aged 11–12 and older.



🔶 A Visitor Out of Time 🤖
An experiment goes wrong, and artificial intelligence X-31 finds itself far in the past — in an old barn where a lost soul roams. Deprived of energy and contact with the base, X-31 must find new support, learn to listen to the unseen, and make a choice that will change more than one fate.
Recommended for readers aged 12-13 and older.



🔶 X-31’s First Assignment 🌵
After an accidental time shift, artificial intelligence X-31 arrives at the YarnTale estate and must search for a host among its inhabitants. Observing their lives, X-31 makes its first choice — a decision that will shape everything that follows.
Recommended for readers aged 12-13 and older.



🔶 A Journey Through Time 🦊
Anastasia travels through time together with Pippa, Liumi, and X-31, where past, present, and future intertwine in vivid episodes of human history. They visit a university classroom of the late twentieth century, a city at war, and a peaceful park by Pushkin’s monument — discovering how differently life sounds across eras.
Recommended for readers aged 12-13 and older.



🔶 A Detective Story — The Attempt on Karl-Magnus 🪳
A strange assassination attempt takes place at the YarnTale estate, with cactus-blogger Karl-Magnus as its target. Behind the seemingly absurd events lie hunger, manipulation, deceit, and deeply human motives.
Recommended for readers aged 13-14 and older.



🔶 Fateful Discovery 🐦‍⬛
Agatha the Crow is clever, cautious, and never trusts a stranger. But one chance meeting in a quiet rural place changes everything — one glance, one word, and history takes a turn that cannot be undone.
Recommended for readers aged 14-15 and older.



🔶 Thorns and Wings: A Psychological Waltz ❤️
In a duel of intellects and illusions, an honest gaze wounds pride. Yet from this pain grows the flower of true self-knowledge.
Recommended for readers aged 14-15 and older.
